Choosing a Lucky Wedding Date

By 270270 EditorialUpdated {TODAY}6 min read

For many Chinese families, the date matters as much as the venue. Here's how couples, and their parents, think about it.

Popular lucky dates

  • 20 May (5.20): 我爱你, "I love you". Marriage registries in China see huge queues.
  • 21 May (5.21): 我愿意, "I do".
  • Qixi (7th day of the 7th lunar month): Chinese Valentine's Day, based on the legend of the Cowherd and the Weaver Girl.
  • Dates containing 8 (prosperity), 9 (久, lasting) or 6 (smooth).
  • Symbolic years: 2014 (爱你一世, "love you for a lifetime") triggered a wedding boom.
{AD:inArticle}

Dates to avoid

  • Ghost Month: the 7th lunar month, when spirits are believed to roam. Qixi itself is the romantic exception.
  • Days with 4 (死), e.g. the 4th, 14th and 24th.
  • Many families also avoid the Qingming festival period and the year's inauspicious days in the Tong Shu almanac (通胜).
