The 270270 Story: A Number of Pairs

By 270270 EditorialUpdated {TODAY}5 min read

270270 doesn't appear in any slang dictionary. That's exactly why we love it: it's a clean slate, built on the one idea every Chinese speaker agrees on: 好事成双, good things come in pairs.

How it sounds

In Mandarin, 270270 is èr qī líng, èr qī líng (二七零，二七零). Folk readings people enjoy:

  • 易起灵 (yì qǐ líng): "easy rise, bright spirit", said twice.
  • 爱妻你 (ài qī nǐ): using internet-slang values (2 = love, as in 520; 7 = wife, 妻; 0 = you, 你), it reads "love · wife · you". Romantic, if you squint.
  • No 4 anywhere: no 死 to worry about.

Why doubled numbers feel lucky

Pairs mean harmony in Chinese culture: couplets on doors, double happiness (囍) at weddings, gifts in even numbers. Mirror numbers like ABCABC are prized in phone numbers and plates because they're memorable and balanced.

The maths is surprisingly beautiful

  • 270270 = 270 × 1001 = 2 · 3³ · 5 · 7 · 11 · 13. Any six-digit ABCABC number is divisible by 7, 11 and 13, because 1001 = 7 × 11 × 13.
  • It has 128 divisors, a power of two, and a divisor sum of 967,680.
  • 270 is a harmonic divisor number (an Ore number), a rare class.
  • An asteroid carries the number: (270270) 2001 UT172.
  • The colour #270270 is a deep royal indigo. It's the colour of the contact bar at the top of every page.
