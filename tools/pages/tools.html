Lucky Price Finder

Enter your current price. We suggest the nearest prices that Chinese shoppers read as prosperous, with no 4s.

Why lucky pricing works

Western retail ends in .99. In Chinese-speaking markets, menus and price tags end in 8 (发), and bundles are priced 168 (一路发) or 888. A price with 4 in it, or a gift of 250 (二百五, "idiot"), sends the wrong message.
